Version 0.1.14 · Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 15+

Set up once.

  1. Install the app

    Open the downloaded .dmg, drag E3 Scribe into Applications, then open it.

  2. Sign in with E3

    Use your @e3group.ai Google account. You’ll automatically join the E3 organization.

  3. Allow Mac permissions

    Follow the prompts for microphone, system audio and Accessibility access. Reopen Scribe if macOS asks.

  4. Connect your meetings

    Connect Google Meet or Slack in the welcome screen. You can manage these later in Settings → Meetings.

Then you’re set.

Scribe runs in the background and is configured to launch at login. Supported detected meetings start recording automatically and stop when the meeting ends.

Original audio and transcripts are shared with E3 and linked to the person and meeting. Find them in Meeting library.

Future updates arrive through the app. Your recording and sharing preferences remain under your control.

Check your first meeting

Join a short Meet or Slack huddle, confirm Scribe starts recording, then leave. Check Meeting library after the upload and transcription finish.

Does my Mac support this?

Open Apple menu → About This Mac. You need an Apple M-series chip and macOS 15 or newer. This installer does not support Intel Macs.
