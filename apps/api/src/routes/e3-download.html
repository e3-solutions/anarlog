Your first setup.

  1. Install and open Scribe

    Run the command above in Terminal. It installs the app and opens setup.

    Or open the downloaded .dmg, drag E3 Scribe into Applications, then open it. Choose Get started.

  2. Sign in with Google

    Choose Sign in with Google and use your @e3group.ai account.

    Approve read-only Google Meet and Calendar access when Google asks. One sign-in connects both and selects your main E3 calendar.

  3. Allow Mac permissions

    Follow each prompt in Scribe, then approve the matching macOS request.

    MicrophoneAllow access to capture your voice.
    System audioAllow access to capture other speakers.
    AccessibilityTurn on E3 Scribe in System Settings so it can detect meeting activity.

    Reopen Scribe if macOS asks.

  4. Finish setup

    Check that Google shows Connected, then choose Finish setup.

    Google is already connected from your first sign-in. No additional account connection is needed.

You’re ready for your next meeting.

Keep Scribe running. It launches at login and records supported detected meetings automatically. Your recordings and transcripts appear in Meeting library. Original audio and transcripts are saved to E3’s database.

Check your first meeting

Join a short Google Meet, confirm Scribe starts recording, then leave. Open Meeting library once the upload finishes.

Do I need an API key?

No. E3 manages shared meeting transcription. You don’t need a personal API key or a model download.

What about updates?

Future updates arrive through the app. Your recording and sharing preferences stay under your control.
