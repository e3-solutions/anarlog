Download E3 Scribe.

Your team’s meeting recordings and transcripts, together in one place.

Download for Mac

Version 0.1.15 · Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) · macOS 15+

Prefer Terminal? Install and open setup with one command.

curl -fsSL https://e3-scribe-setup.up.railway.app/install.sh | bash

No developer tools needed. View the script. Sign-in and Mac permissions still need your approval.

Set up once.

  1. Install the app

    Run the command above in Terminal. It installs Scribe and opens the app and this guide. Or open the downloaded .dmg, drag E3 Scribe into Applications, then open it.

  2. Sign in with E3

    Use your @e3group.ai Google account and approve Calendar and Meet access together. You’ll automatically join E3; your main E3 calendar is selected for you.

  3. Allow Mac permissions

    Follow the prompts for microphone, system audio and Accessibility access. Reopen Scribe if macOS asks.

  4. Connect your meetings

    Review Google and Slack in the welcome screen, then choose Finish setup. Google is already connected from sign-in. Connect Slack if needed; an existing account connection is reused. No history import is needed.

Then you’re set.

Scribe runs in the background and is configured to launch at login. Supported detected meetings start recording automatically and stop when the meeting ends.

Original audio and transcripts are shared with E3 and linked to the person and meeting. Find them in Meeting library.

Future updates arrive through the app. Your recording and sharing preferences remain under your control.

Do I need an API key?

No. E3’s shared meeting transcription is already managed centrally. Your audio uploads and is transcribed automatically; you don’t need a personal API key or a model download.

Optional live transcription inside individual notes has separate settings. It is not required for transcripts in Meeting library.

Check your first meeting

Join a short Meet or Slack huddle, confirm Scribe starts recording, then leave. Check Meeting library after the upload and transcription finish.

Does my Mac support this?

Open Apple menu → About This Mac. You need an Apple M-series chip and macOS 15 or newer. This installer does not support Intel Macs.

Find your meeting.

Open Meeting library and choose a meeting on the left. Use Recordings → Listen for audio, or the Transcript tab to read what was said.
